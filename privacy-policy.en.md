# Clear Share Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Clear Share is provided by **Softutopia**. This policy explains the current iPhone app's handling of information.

## Your expense records stay local

Clear Share does not require an account. Group and member names, expense descriptions and amounts, shares, repayments, change history, personal preferences, and reminder schedules are stored on your device. The app processes balances and statements there.

This version does not upload your ledger or preferences to a Clear Share sync server. Other group members do not automatically receive your changes. We have not added advertising, tracking, or an analytics service to the app.

## Personal reminders

Notifications are optional. When you allow them and create a reminder, iOS schedules it on your device. Reminders are not messages to group members. A reminder's title and content may appear on the Lock Screen according to your iOS settings. You can manage reminders in the app and notification permission in iOS Settings.

## Statements, exports, and imported files

The app can create PDF statements and JSON backups locally. These files can include names, expense amounts, repayments, and history. Clear Share does not encrypt these exports or automatically send them to us.

You choose the recipient, app, or storage location through the system share sheet. The destination you select may store or transmit the copy under its own rules. Choose a trusted destination and protect exported files.

Importing a backup requires you to select a file. The app validates it before restoring records. When the existing records are readable, it keeps a local safety copy before replacement. A PDF statement is not a restorable backup.

## Device backups and Apple services

Your iOS backup settings may cause app data to be included in a device backup. Apple's distribution and testing services may provide diagnostics or TestFlight feedback under your Apple settings and Apple's policies. This is separate from the app's local ledger; Clear Share does not run its own automatic cloud backup or crash-reporting service.

## If you contact support

When you contact [contact@softutopia.com](mailto:contact@softutopia.com), we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Do not send a full ledger, statement, or backup unless it is needed for the request.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Our email provider also processes messages to deliver the correspondence. This does not give us automatic access to records inside your app.

## Your choices and removing data

You control the records and exports you create. Local change history may remain after an expense is edited or removed so that earlier records can be reviewed or recovered.

To remove the app's local records, use **Delete App** in iOS rather than **Offload App**, which can keep documents and data. Removing the app does not remove copies you already exported, messages you sent, or device backups. Manage those copies separately where they were saved. [Apple's storage guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108429)

## Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy when the app's information handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy or support questions, contact [contact@softutopia.com](mailto:contact@softutopia.com).
